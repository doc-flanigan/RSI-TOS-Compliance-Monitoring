### 2. www.starcitizenstore.com — Domain Name Violation (Trademark + Commercial Use)

**Why it's critical:**
- The domain "starcitizenstore.com" uses the RSI trademark "Star Citizen" combined with "store."
- "Star Citizen Store" is the natural language description of RSI's own official shop — this domain is likely to confuse consumers and misrepresent affiliation.
- RSI has historically issued DMCA and trademark notices to fan operations using similar commercial-sounding names.
- If any goods (merchandise, game packages, accounts) are sold under this domain, this is a direct RSI ToS and trademark violation.

**Recommended actions (in priority order):**
1. Same as buystarcitizen.com — consult a trademark attorney.
2. If goods are being sold: cease immediately pending legal review.
3. Contact RSI/CIG for written guidance.
4. Strongly consider rebranding to a domain that does not incorporate RSI trademarks.
